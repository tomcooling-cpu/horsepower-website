About this plan

This is the plan for your first duathlon. Around 7 hours a week across 12 weeks, built to take you from wherever you are now to a start line you have never seen before, calm, prepared and ready to finish strong. Nothing is assumed and nothing is skipped. Every session tells you exactly what to do and why, in plain language.
