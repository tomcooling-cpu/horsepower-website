What this page covers

This page covers horsepowercoaching.co.uk only. When you buy a plan you are taken to TrainingPeaks, and when you message me on WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook you are using those services. Each of them has its own privacy policy, and I don't control what they collect.

The contact form

When you send the form on the contact page I receive your name, your email address, your target event if you give one, and your message. I use them to reply to you and, if we go on to work together, to set up your coaching. The form is run by Netlify, which hosts this site, so your message is stored on Netlify's systems and emailed to me. I keep enquiries for as long as I need them to deal with your question, and I delete them when they are no longer needed. The legal basis is that you have asked me to get in touch, and my legitimate interest in answering enquiries about my coaching.

Measurement with Google Analytics

If you click Accept on the cookie banner, this site runs Google Analytics 4. It tells me which pages people read, which plans they look at, which buttons they click, how far down a page they get, whether they start and send the contact form, and whether a visit came from a Google ad. I use that to improve the site and to see whether the advertising I pay for is worth it.

Google Analytics sets cookies on your device and sends data about your visit to Google, including a shortened version of your IP address, your browser and device type and the pages you view. Google acts on my instructions for the analytics itself, and it also uses the data under its own terms. I have switched off Google's cross-device signals and personalised advertising features. Analytics data is kept in my Google Analytics account for 14 months and then deleted.

Nothing is sent to Google unless you accept. If you reject, or make no choice, Google Analytics does not load and no analytics cookies are set. You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. The legal basis is your consent.

I never send your name, email address or the contents of your message to Google Analytics.
