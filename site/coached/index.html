Honest answers

Is there a call every week?

No. Plan Only is built around the work and the why, delivered in writing so you can go back to it. If you want calls, WhatsApp and race-day strategy built together, that is Coached by Tom.

How often do you review my training?

Once a month. Each cycle is three weeks of training then a rest week, and the rest week is when I review the block, write your feedback and build the next one. There is no per-session feedback in between.

What if my numbers change or life gets in the way?

The plan moves. When your data shifts or a week falls apart, the next block reflects it. That is the point of building it block by block instead of all at once.

Which sports do you coach?

Triathlon across every distance, road and ultra running, and cycling from sportives to ultra-distance. If you have a target event, I can build for it.

Can I message you between blocks?

Leave notes on your sessions in TrainingPeaks and I read every one in the rest week, as part of the block review. There is no back and forth in between. If you want my time day to day, that is Coached by Tom.

Is TrainingPeaks Premium included?

No. Plan Only is delivered to your own TrainingPeaks account. A free TrainingPeaks Premium account comes with Coached by Tom.

Is there a minimum term?

Yes. Plan Only has a three-month minimum, the same as Coached by Tom. After those first three months it runs month to month, and either of us can end it by giving at least one month's notice in writing. An email is fine. Three months is three full cycles of training, review and the next block built from what you actually did, which is enough time for the plan to show you what it does.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. If you want me directly, you can move to Coached by Tom when a place is open.

Where it differs from full coaching

There are no scheduled calls and I am not on call day to day. What you get is a programme I build around your life and your event, with one proper review a month: at the end of every three week block, in the rest week, I go through the sessions you actually did and build the next block from them. If you want me in your corner for all of it, look at Coached by Tom.
